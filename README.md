# The Laminated Plant

Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) — one line of office comedy, at most 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The joke

> Our office plant died, so Facilities replaced it with a laminated photo of a plant and a recurring calendar invite to water it.

127 characters. The laugh is earned in the last few words: a dead plant, a laminated photo of a plant, and — the twist — a recurring calendar invite to water a photograph. Specific office details throughout (Facilities, the laminated photo, the calendar invite), no word wasted.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
